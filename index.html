    How Confam works

    Five places searched, two promises kept, and one habit we want you to build: send it to someone before you pay.

    What we check

    • TikTokVideos and comments
    • XPosts and replies
    • Instagram and FacebookComments and call-outs
    • Nairaland and the webThreads and forums
    • Official recordsBank account name, CAC business search, SEC operator list

    What we never do

    We never say a vendor is safe, and we never call anyone a scammer. A clean result means nothing turned up in the places we searched.

    We hide the middle of account numbers. We do not keep posts their author deleted. We never charge vendors.
